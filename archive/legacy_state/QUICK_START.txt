Q: How do I start trading?
A: 1) python safe_start.py (verify everything)
   2) python main.py (start bot)
   3) Monitor logs/trades.csv for results

Q: What if I have orphaned positions?
A: Close manually in MT5 Terminal → Positions → Right-click → Close Position
   The bot cannot manage positions from previous sessions.

A: Run "python safe_start.py" - it auto-detects and repairs JSON corruption
   This was a problem from 50 days offline (62 corrupted files found).

Q: Can I trade other pairs?

   pairs will result in losses. The 12 disabled pairs all have negative
   historical win rates for a reason.

Q: Can I increase lot size?
A: NOT RECOMMENDED. Current 0.01 is optimized for $300-500 account.
   - 0.01 lot = $1.50 risk per trade
   - 0.02 lot = $3.00 risk per trade (doubles losses)
   Wait until account grows to $5K+ before increasing.

Q: What if it loses money?
A: Normal variance. Worst case: -$10/day (2% loss). Circuit breaker stops
   at 5% account loss. Review losing trades in logs/trades.csv to understand.
   Most bad days recover within 2-3 days.
