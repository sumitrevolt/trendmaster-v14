## What this proves (and what it does NOT)

**It proves:** the gates correctly identify and refuse to enter the worst conditions
generated by the synthetic series (chop regime, wide spread, off-hours). On this
data the baseline takes 7 candidate signals across the 3 seeds; the gated arm
filters them all and the baseline ends -$3.33 / -0.33R while the gated arm sits
flat at $1000.00.

**It does NOT prove** that the gates raise expectancy on real broker data. The
synthetic generator is intentionally hostile (60% trend / 30% chop / 10% spike)
and the H4-stride decision loop produces few candidates per seed. With trades=0
in the gated arm, the "+0.167R per trade" delta is mathematically vacuous — it
just means we lost no money by not trading.

## Honest read

The right interpretation: **filters work as designed (skip bad conditions, preserve
capital).** Validating *positive* expectancy requires walk-forward retraining on
captured live `trades.csv`, exactly as called out in `PROFITABILITY_PLAYBOOK.md`:

